# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers, indie makers, and solopreneurs who want a clean, fast blog on their own custom domain. They value simplicity, speed, and owning their content. They are technical enough to appreciate Markdown and custom domains, but not interested in maintaining WordPress.

## Product Purpose

IncBlog is a white-label SaaS blogging platform. Each user gets their own blog served from a custom domain. The product eliminates the friction of setting up and running a personal blog — no server management, no theme maintenance, no plugin updates. Write in Markdown, publish instantly.

## Brand Personality

Focused, clean, confident. The product speaks plainly — no buzzwords, no fluff. Think "the blogging tool that gets out of your way."

## Anti-references

- WordPress.com marketing page (cluttered, feature-bloating)
- Medium (corporate, impersonal)
- Ghost marketing (dark, moody, overly branded)
- Generic SaaS landing pages with cream backgrounds, gradient CTAs, and identical card grids

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product, don't describe it** — let the editor, the speed, the clean design speak for themselves
2. **One action per viewport** — the visitor should always know what to do next
3. **Fast is a feature** — the page itself must feel instant, no bloated scripts or heavy assets
4. **Own your words** — the copy should feel direct and human, not marketing-generated

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance for contrast and interactive elements
- Semantic HTML throughout
- Keyboard navigable
- Reduced motion support via prefers-reduced-motion
